Robert Fader · Premonition

Chapter 2: Vertigo

✦

Robert raced through the deserted streets on his motorcycle, feeling the wind whistle past his ears and the vibration of the engine pass through his entire body. Usually the freedom he felt while riding was a source of inspiration and strength. But today something was wrong. Without warning, a wave of dizziness began to wash over his consciousness. The ground seemed to sway beneath him, and the world around him lost its sharpness, its edges, its certainty.

With every yard he traveled, the fear tightening around him grew stronger. He tried to calm himself and focus on the road, but his efforts were useless. At last Robert decided to stop. He looked around quickly. The empty streets were almost lifeless, and only the dull glow of the streetlamps flickered in his eyes.

When he noticed a group of armed men who looked openly threatening, he understood that panic would not help him. Gathering all his courage, Robert decided to approach, careful not to reveal his unease. The men stiffened as soon as they saw him coming.

— “Hey. Who are you?” one of them asked, slowly raising his weapon.

— “My name is Robert. I want to talk,” he replied, trying to keep his voice steady and confident.

Robert offered them the idea of building a city for survivors — a safe place for people, far from every danger. His words were met with loud laughter. The leader of the group, Alexey, looked at him with a mocking expression.

— “A city? Seriously? In this world?”

Alexey stood out among the others not only because of his physique, but because of the way he carried himself. Tall and broad, with neatly trimmed blond hair, he inspired both fear and respect. His clothes were of better quality than those of the other men, who looked neglected and wore rags. Alexey had become a symbol of strength and cruelty in this merciless world. His confident gaze and upright posture underlined his status as leader.

Although Robert was trembling inside, he did not give in. He explained that he had a plan, resources, and people willing to help. The men exchanged glances, and Alexey proposed that they test what Robert was capable of. He suggested a duel with swords.

Robert, knowing that he was a skilled fencer, agreed. The duel began, and he focused. His heart beat in rhythm with the combat. The sword fight erupted in a thunderous clash of steel as the blades met. With a concentrated and determined gaze, Robert faced his first opponent — a strong, aggressive man. Gracefully avoiding the brutal blow, Robert seized the opening and struck with precision, knocking the sword from the man’s hand. The weapon flew into the air, and the man himself, stunned, fell to the ground, unable to withstand Robert’s skill.

The second opponent, more cunning, tried to overwhelm him with a series of rapid attacks. But Robert moved with an almost supernatural calm, anticipating every strike. In a single elegant turn, with the faintest touch of his blade, he made the second man’s sword slip from his hand. Disoriented, the man stumbled and fell, unable to keep pace with the rhythm of the fight.

The third opponent was clearly drunk. He approached unsteadily, wearing a foolish smile. Robert, with a mixture of compassion and amusement, decided not to harm him. With a simple movement of his sword, he diverted the drunk man’s clumsy attack. The man lost his balance and fell onto his back, laughing as he went down. Robert smiled, understanding that victory sometimes came not only through strength, but through wit and the ability to read one’s opponents.

Robert’s sword was a true work of art. This elegant double-edged sabre, as if drawn from a medieval legend, had a graceful leather-wrapped hilt and a curved blade that gleamed in the dim lamplight. Every line and curve had been considered with care, giving it not only aesthetic beauty but extraordinary effectiveness in battle. The blade was razor-sharp, and its double edge allowed Robert to strike from either side, making him a dangerous opponent on any field of combat.

When only Alexey remained, he stepped forward, his eyes burning with resolve. Robert understood that this would be the decisive moment. They exchanged blows, and the tension rose. Then Robert used an unexpected maneuver that threw Alexey off balance, sending him crashing to the ground.

Standing over his defeated opponent, Robert said:

— “I don’t like violence. I don’t want it to become part of our future.”

Surprised, Alexey rose to his knees and acknowledged Robert’s superiority. Now that a mutual respect had been established, Robert began to explain how he saw the future of the city.

Impressed by his skill, the leader raised his hands in surrender.

— “All right. You have proved your strength. We can talk about your plans.”

Feeling a surge of confidence, Robert began to explain how they could unite their resources and knowledge to build a fortress where people could live in peace and help one another. The men listened with interest, and Alexey, now more open to Robert’s ideas, said:

— “If you are truly prepared to work with us, we will help you. But understand this: we will be watching to make sure everything goes according to plan. Do not disappoint us.”

Robert felt an incredible sense of relief and gratitude.

— “Thank you. I won’t let you down. Together we can achieve far more than any of us could alone.”

The men dispersed, leaving Robert alone with the silence of the night city. He drew a deep breath, feeling the tension begin to ease. Yet even now, after everything that had happened, he could not rid himself of that sense of instability.

— “We have to act quickly,” he thought, looking around. “Time is not on our side.”

Robert returned to his motorcycle, feeling the dizziness begin to fade. He knew that many trials still lay ahead, but he also understood that this first step might change everything. The virtual shape of his plan was beginning to weave itself into reality, and he felt that his mission had only just begun.
