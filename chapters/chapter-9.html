Robert Fader · Premonition

Chapter 9: Passions

✦

Night wrapped Our Utopia in a soft veil, and the stars shone brightly in the cloudless sky. Full of enthusiasm, Oksana turned to Martin, her voice melodic and playful.

“Martin, show me the tower. I heard the view from there is extraordinary.”

Martin felt a surge of pride and nodded.

“Of course. I would be happy to show you. It is one of the best places in the fortress.”

They walked toward the staircase leading up the tower. Martin felt his heart beat faster with excitement. He wanted to impress Oksana and show her that he was not merely a warrior, but a man capable of romance and care.

When they reached the top, Oksana stopped, amazed by the view. The moon illuminated the surroundings, and the stars shimmered like jewels scattered across the black velvet of the sky.

“It is incredible!” she exclaimed, turning to Martin. “I have never seen anything like it.”

Martin, proud that he had impressed her, smiled.

“Yes, it is beautiful here. But that is not all I wanted to show you.”

He approached the wall where a rocket launcher had been installed after the recent battle with the savages.

“This is a weapon,” he said, pointing to it. “We used it to repel the savages’ attack. It was necessary to protect our home.”

Oksana, listening carefully, suddenly froze. Her face changed, and shock appeared in her eyes.

“You... you killed people?” she said, her voice trembling. “You are a murderer.”

Martin had not expected such a reaction and was thrown off balance.

“I... I did it to protect us. We could not simply sit and wait for them to attack.”

“That does not justify killing!” Oksana cried, her voice becoming sharp. “Do you not understand what you did? You became part of the cycle of violence we are all trying to break.”

At that moment Kenneth approached them, having noticed that the atmosphere was growing heated. He saw Oksana looking distressed, while Martin seemed confused and angry.

“What is going on here?” Kenneth asked, his voice wary.

“She is accusing me of murder,” Martin replied, irritation filling his tone. “I was defending our fortress.”

Kenneth understood that the situation was slipping out of control and stepped between them.

“Martin, you have to understand that your actions have consequences. We cannot afford to lose our humanity, even while fighting to survive.”

“I cannot take the risk,” Kenneth said, his eyes flashing with determination. “If you do not step away from her, I will be forced to fight you.”

Martin refused to yield. He drew his sword.

“Try it, Kenneth. I am not afraid of you.”

Kenneth, realizing that decisive action might be necessary, drew his own sword.

“I do not want to fight you. But if you do not stop, you will leave me no choice.”

Tension hung in the air, and both men took fighting stances, ready for a clash. Oksana watched in horror, realizing that their conflict could lead to unpredictable consequences.

Kenneth, no longer willing to wait, stepped forward and tried to seize Martin by the shoulder.

“You do not understand what you are doing,” he said, his voice full of warning.

But Martin, refusing to give way, spun around and struck Kenneth in the face. The blow was strong, and Kenneth, caught off guard by such aggression, lost his balance. He nearly fell from the edge of the tower, but managed to grab the railing, his heart pounding with fear.

Oksana stood in complete confusion. She did not know what to do.

“Martin, stop!” she cried, her voice trembling with anxiety. “This is not the way. We cannot let hatred destroy what we are trying to build.”

But Martin, having lost control, merely grinned. His eyes flashed with rage, and he seemed almost to enjoy the situation.

“You think you can stop me?” he said, his voice threatening. “I am not going to give up.”

Kenneth, still clinging to the railing, tried to pull himself up, but his face showed fear and bewilderment.

“Martin, you have gone mad. This is not you.”

At that moment Sasha, who was below, heard the shouting and the noise. She lifted her head and saw the conflict erupting on the tower. Her heart beat faster when she noticed Kenneth hanging at the edge, while Martin seemed ready to attack again.

“Martin!” she shouted, her voice firm. “What is happening? Calm down.”

Sasha immediately understood that Martin had lost control. If he did not stop, the situation could end tragically. She hurried up the stairs, her thoughts full of alarm.

“Martin, do not do this!” she cried when she reached the upper platform. “You cannot let hatred rule you.”

Hearing her voice, Martin froze for a moment, but then his face twisted with anger.

“Get away, Sasha. This is none of your business.”

Kenneth, still holding onto the railing, tried to get to his feet.

“Martin, this is not the way. We are all here to build something better, not to destroy one another.”

Sasha, realizing that the situation was becoming more dangerous, stepped closer.

“Martin, listen to me. We have all lived through too much to let this conflict destroy us. You cannot kill your friends.”

But Martin seemed not to hear her. His eyes burned with fury, and he was ready to do anything to prove he was right. Oksana, standing aside, felt her heart tighten with fear. She could not believe she had found herself in such a situation, and her voice trembled.

“Please, stop,” she cried, her words sounding like a call for help. “We can solve this peacefully.”

The situation grew hotter, and the tension in the air became unbearable. Sasha knew that if they did not stop Martin, everything might end in tragedy. She took a step forward, ready to intervene, but in her heart there grew the feeling that all of them were standing on the edge of an abyss, and one wrong step could bring disaster.
