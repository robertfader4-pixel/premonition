Robert Fader · Premonition

Chapter 12: Mystery and Danger

✦

Luis, Natalia, and Robert were hiding in a secret room known only to Luis, the architect of the fortress. The room was small but comfortable, with dim light slipping through cracks in the walls. Natalia was terrified. She had only wanted to play music, and now she found herself at the center of a conspiracy.

— “This has never happened here before,” Luis explained, his voice serious. “They have staged a coup. The main traitor is Martin.”

Robert suddenly lost consciousness again. Luis, seizing the moment, decided to confess what he had been holding inside.

— “I am your greatest fan, Natalia. I know everything about you.”

Natalia was bewildered. Everything seemed unbearably tangled in this post-apocalyptic world.

— “What are we supposed to do?” she asked, her voice trembling with anxiety.

— “We have to stop Martin,” Luis replied sadly, since his confession had not moved Natalia. “But it will not be easy. Martin is a former soldier, a war hero who fought before the Great Nuclear Catastrophe. He knows how to act in situations like this.”

Meanwhile, Robert, half asleep, began to receive visions. He saw a vast army approaching Our Utopia. This time he could see them more clearly. He began to rave, describing the army and its flag.

— “That is… that is the flag of the Necrologists,” Natalia said, her heart beating faster. She recognized the description Robert was giving.

Luis gave a faint smirk.

— “How interesting, Natalia. Robert and I share one passion. Natalia. Only this Robert has a double passion, ha-ha.”

Natalia did not fully understand Luis’s words. But when Robert began to describe his wife, she realized he was speaking of the great nurse from Necrology: the cardiologist with red hair and light eyes. Natalia, the cellist, was shocked. Robert’s premonition was real. She understood that a great danger existed.

Luis explained to Natalia that there were three problems:

Robert was in a trance.

Our Utopia had been seized by Zhenya and Martin.

An army was moving toward them, and they did not know what to do.

Natalia felt fear closing around her and understood that time was running out. They had to act before it was too late.

Gathering her thoughts, Natalia looked at Luis.

— “We need a plan. If Martin and Zhenya have taken control of Our Utopia, we cannot simply sit here and wait for the army to arrive.”

Luis nodded, his face turning grave.

— “You’re right. But how can we stop them if Robert is in no condition to help?”

Robert, still trapped between sleep and wakefulness, began to rave again.

— “They are coming… with a flag… yellow, with a snake drawn on it…”

Natalia felt a chill run down her spine.

— “What does that mean? Why is he talking about a flag?”

— “It is the flag of the savages,” Luis said quietly. “They are known for their cruelty. If they truly are coming here together with the Necrologists, it could mean the end for all of us.”

Trying to master herself, Natalia said:

— “We must find a way to regain control of the fortress. If we can stop Martin, perhaps we can prevent the attack.”

Luis thought for a moment.

— “I have a few ideas, but we need time to prepare. We can use the secret passages to reach the center of the fortress and gather the people who are still loyal to Robert.”

Luis felt a subtle excitement at the thought that Natalia was beginning to trust him.

— “And what about Robert?” Natalia asked, looking at him as he remained in the trance. “He cannot stay like this.”

— “I’ll try to wake him,” Luis said, bending over Robert. “Robert, can you hear me? We need your help.”

As though he had heard him, Robert slowly began to open his eyes.

— “Natalia…” he whispered, his voice weak. “I saw them… they are coming…”

— “I know,” Natalia answered, leaning closer to him. “But we have to act. You must help us stop Martin and Zhenya.”

Gathering his strength, Robert nodded.

— “I… I will try. Natalia… Natalia… I adore your name…”

Luis looked at Robert with disapproval.

— “We have to split up,” Robert said, standing. “I will go find Martin and stop him. Luis, you stay here with Natalia and keep watch. If anything goes wrong, let me know.”

— “I will gladly remain with this beautiful lady, Robert,” Luis said with a sly smile.

Natalia, feeling fear tighten around her again, replied:

— “Be careful, Robert. We do not know what may happen.”

— “I will,” he said with confidence. “I will not let them destroy what we have built.”

Robert left the room and headed toward the central square. Through the secret passages he reached the main plaza. As soon as he arrived, he found a crowd of people gathered, waiting for news. He raised his hand to draw their attention.

— “People of Our Utopia!” he began, his voice firm. “We are in danger. Martin and Zhenya have seized our fortress, and we must unite to stop the conspirators.”

The crowd fell silent, and tension hung in the air. Some began whispering among themselves, but Robert continued.

— “We cannot allow them to destroy what we have built together. We must act now, before it is too late.”

A voice rose from the crowd.

— “But how can we deal with them? They are stronger than we are.”

Without losing confidence, Robert answered:

— “We are stronger when we act together. We have the spirit of unity and the will to protect our home. If we combine our strength, we can win.”

The crowd began to stir, and Robert felt hope returning. People started to rise, ready to fight for their freedom.

Seeing the crowd awaken, Robert continued speaking, trying to inspire them to act.

— “We must unite and find Martin,” he cried. “He cannot continue to rule our fortress and threaten our lives.”

Voices of support rang out from among the people, and a tall bearded man stepped forward.

— “I heard that Martin gathered his supporters in the old warehouse on the eastern side of the fortress,” he said confidently. “They are planning their next actions against us.”

Others began to confirm it.

— “Yes, I heard the same thing,” added a woman with long hair. “They plan to use the warehouse as a base for their attacks.”

Hearing this, Robert thanked them.

— “Thank you. We must go there at once and stop him before it is too late.”

Inspired by his words, the crowd gathered around him, ready to follow their leader. Robert looked at them, and his confidence grew.

— “Let us show Martin that we are united,” he shouted. “We will not let him destroy what we built together.”

With those words, Robert led the crowd toward the warehouse, determined to fight for their freedom and take back control of Our Utopia. A decisive battle awaited them, and he was certain that together they could overcome any obstacle.

At last, Robert came face to face with Martin and Zhenya.

— “So the utopian finally appears,” Martin said with contempt.

Seeing them, Robert asked why they had done it.

— “You have violated the very foundations of Our Utopia. You have unleashed violence in the most humane place on earth,” he said, looking at them with condemnation.

In response, Zhenya declared that Robert was a fool and did not understand how important it was to live in prosperity.
