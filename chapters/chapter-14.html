Robert Fader · Premonition

Chapter 14: Storm in the Heart

✦

Robert seized his sword and felt adrenaline flood his body. Martin, fury burning in his eyes, also took up his weapon and, without wasting a second, rushed at him with his blade raised. An epic duel began — two powerful warriors colliding, each determined to prevail.

Chaos flared around them. Robert noticed Oksana nearby, bound hand and foot not far from the place where they were fighting. Hope flashed in his heart. With a swift sweep of his sword, he cut through the ropes that held her.

— “Oksana, run!” he shouted as she was freed.

Oksana wasted no time and rushed toward the exit. She knew there were other prisoners inside the fortress who needed her help, and she could not leave them to their fate.

Meanwhile, Kenneth, Sasha, and other residents of Our Utopia were freed. They quickly gathered, ready to fight. At that moment, Zhenya, seeing her plan collapse, attacked Sasha, but Sasha defended herself with confidence.

— “You will not stop us,” Sasha said, her voice firm.

Zhenya hurled insults at the residents of the fortress, calling them beggars and failures.

Kenneth saw Zhenya throw herself at Sasha and intervened without hesitation. He seized Zhenya firmly by the arm, helping Sasha restrain her. Together they managed to bring Zhenya down to the floor and pin her securely to the ground. At that instant, Oksana ran toward Kenneth and embraced him, expressing her gratitude for his support. Watching them, Sasha felt a wave of jealousy. Her heart beat faster, and her mood shifted sharply, leaving her unsure how to handle the emotions rising inside her.

Robert and Martin, meanwhile, continued to fight. Every stroke of their swords was full of rage. Martin, using all his strength, wounded Robert in the shoulder. Pain shot through Robert, but he had no intention of surrendering.

At the same time, Natalia played the cello, filling the air with music and creating an atmosphere of hope. She was performing I, Dissolved in Your Soul, and her melody filled the hearts of the people with strength and inspiration.

Suddenly Luis, standing behind Natalia, leaned down and kissed her. Natalia did not resist; she continued playing, and her music became more emotional and powerful as Luis kissed her.

Martin took advantage of the moment and suddenly dealt Robert a nearly fatal blow, wounding him in the neck. Robert felt life draining from him, but he understood that he had to fight to the end.

— “Ask Natalia to play Premonition!” he shouted. “Now!”

Kenneth heard the cry and rushed toward Natalia. He noticed Luis passionately kissing her, but chose to ignore it. The only thing that mattered was that Natalia help Robert.

— “Oh, my God!” he exclaimed when he saw what Luis was doing with Natalia while she played. But without losing time, he turned to her. “Natalia, play Premonition. Robert needs it.”

Natalia felt the tension in the air. She looked at Luis and finally said:

— “Wait. I need to pause.”

She took a deep breath and began to play Premonition. The melody, full of mystery and power, filled the space. Hearing it, Robert felt his strength begin to return. The music poured energy into him, and he began to parry Martin’s attacks with renewed confidence.

With every strike, Robert felt his certainty grow. He forced Martin back against the wall and, using everything left in him, gained the upper hand. Soon he defeated him, and Robert’s sword rested at the throat of his fallen enemy.

Drops of Robert’s blood fell onto Martin’s face, and in that moment the crowd understood that the coup had been suppressed. Kenneth embraced Oksana and kissed her, and in that difficult instant they both felt their love grow stronger. Sasha, holding Zhenya — who had realized that her shared plan with Martin had collapsed — felt sadness and defeat. She tied Zhenya firmly, preventing her from breaking free.

— “We must all unite,” Sasha said. “This is not the end, but a new beginning for all of us.”

The crowd gathered around them began to applaud, realizing that they had won. Robert, still standing with his sword at Martin’s throat, looked at his friends and at the people who had supported them.

— “We cannot allow hatred and division to destroy what we have built,” Robert said, lowering his sword and looking at those assembled. His words filled the atmosphere with hope and unity. “Instead, let us work together to create a better future for everyone.”

At that moment, encouraged by Robert’s words, Kenneth led Martin away. As Martin left, he felt the warmth of the unity being born there. The others, inspired by that atmosphere, began discussing plans for reconstruction and cooperation, understanding that only by joining their efforts could they overcome hardship and build a peaceful future. Everyone felt that a new era was beginning — one free from hatred and destruction.

When Natalia finished playing, she approached Robert and embraced him, feeling his heart beat in rhythm with hers.

— “You were incredible,” she whispered, looking into his eyes.

Luis, standing nearby, felt anger flare inside his chest. It was hard for him to see Natalia so close to Robert, and his jealousy began to boil. He clenched his fists, trying to suppress his emotions, but deep down he felt he was losing her.

— “We were all incredible,” Robert answered, his voice full of gratitude. “It was the work of the entire team.”

Kenneth returned, and he and Oksana approached them.

— “We have to rebuild our fortress and make it a place where everyone feels safe,” Kenneth said, his voice determined.

— “Yes,” Sasha agreed. “We must learn from our mistakes and create a society based on trust and mutual support.”

Feeling the support of her friends, she added:

— “We can begin by freeing everyone who is still captive. We must show that we are united and that Our Utopia can flourish again.”

Inspired by their words, the crowd began discussing how they could help one another and restore Our Utopia.

Looking at his friends and at the people beside him, Robert felt that they truly could create something great. But at that moment Natalia noticed the wounds on his body, and worry twisted her face.

— “Robert, are you all right?” she asked, leaning toward him. “Your wounds need treatment.”

She quickly took medical supplies from her backpack and began carefully tending to his injuries. Instantly, Robert remembered his wife Natalia, who had been a doctor. The memories surged over him, and he felt his heart tighten with pain.

Suddenly, from exhaustion and loss of blood, Robert lost consciousness and fell to the ground. Natalia cried out in panic:

— “Robert!”

Seeing this, Luis felt his jealousy give way to alarm. He rushed toward Robert to help, though a shadow of resentment still remained in his heart.

— “Come on, help him!” he shouted, trying to suppress his feelings. “We have to get him stabilized.”

The crowd, realizing the seriousness of the situation, quickly gathered around, ready to assist. Natalia, focusing on Robert, knew she had to do everything possible to bring him back.

— “Robert, hold on,” she whispered, trying not to give in to panic. She checked his pulse and examined his wounds, knowing that time was against them.

Luis, his heart pounding with fear, came closer and offered his help.

— “I can bring water and something for the bandages,” he said, trying to appear confident.

Natalia nodded without taking her eyes off Robert. She knew that every moment mattered.

— “Quickly, Luis,” she ordered, and he rushed into the crowd to find what was needed.

Kenneth and Oksana, seeing what was happening, began organizing the other residents.

— “We need to make a stretcher so we can move him somewhere safe,” Kenneth shouted, and the people began to act, gathering anything they could use.

Sasha, noticing Natalia’s tension, came up to her and said:

— “You can do this, Natalia. You are strong, and you know what to do.”

Feeling their support, Natalia drew a breath and continued treating Robert’s wounds, trying not to think about what might happen if she failed to act in time.

When Luis returned with water and bandages, Natalia quickly cleaned the wounds and did everything she could to stabilize Robert.

— “Now lift him,” she ordered when she was finished.

United, the crowd carefully raised Robert onto the stretcher and began moving toward a safer place where they could give him more skilled care.

Luis walked beside them, his heart still torn by contradictory feelings, but he understood that the most important thing now was to help Robert.

— “We will do everything we can to make sure he recovers,” he said, looking at Natalia, who remained focused on her task.

Natalia nodded, her worry deepening. She knew they had to stand together in order to save Robert and restore what had become her new home — Our Utopia.
