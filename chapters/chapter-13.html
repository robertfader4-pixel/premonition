Robert Fader · Premonition

Chapter 13: Disappointment

✦

The crowd froze in expectation as the two opponents stood facing one another, ready for the decisive duel that would determine the fate of their fortress and their future.

— “You are a traitor, Robert!” Martin shouted, his voice filled with hatred. “You betrayed us, and now you want to destroy everything we built.”

Robert refused to yield to the provocation. He answered with confidence:

— “I am not a traitor. I am fighting for our freedom and for the future of our people.”

The crowd, feeling the tension in the air, began to whisper in support of Robert. They did not believe Martin’s words. They understood that he was trying to ignite hatred and fear.

— “Come on, show whose side you are on!” one of the residents shouted, raising his fist in support of Robert.

Realizing that his words were no longer working, Martin tightened his grip on the sword and lunged forward, ready to attack. Robert gathered all his strength and met the blow. A brutal duel began — one that would decide the fate of their community.

Every clash of steel sounded like thunder, and the crowd held its breath as they watched a battle that could change their lives forever.
