Robert Fader · Premonition

Chapter 10: Passions II

✦

Sasha approached Martin with the cold metal of a pistol clenched in her hands. Her thoughts were filled with fear and resolve. Kenneth, her friend and comrade, was in danger, and Sasha could not allow herself to lose him. Each step echoed dully inside her chest as she reached the place where Kenneth was.

Suddenly there came a heavy sound, like something falling. Sasha froze, her heart beating faster. She rushed toward the noise and saw Kenneth struggling to rise, then stumbling and falling to the floor. Oksana, standing nearby, was in shock. Her eyes widened in horror as she saw him fall, but fortunately he landed safely and had not been seriously injured.

“Kenneth!” Sasha shouted, running to him. “Are you all right?”

Kenneth, breathing heavily, lifted his head and tried to smile. He nodded. He was clearly exhausted, but in general he seemed well. Sasha helped him stand, and they both turned toward Oksana, who still could not collect herself.

“Oksana, we have to go,” Sasha said, trying to speak calmly.

Oksana nodded, and they began to move together. Sasha noticed how Kenneth looked at Oksana with admiration, and jealousy crept into her heart. She could not understand why it disturbed her so much, but she felt that something was forming between them.

“Are you all right?” Oksana asked Kenneth.

He smiled again, and that only sharpened Sasha’s jealousy.

“Yes, everything is fine,” Kenneth replied, and his voice sounded so warm that Sasha felt her own heart tighten.

Meanwhile Robert appeared in the corridor. His face was focused, his eyes full of determination. He quickly assessed the situation and, without waiting for explanations, approached Martin, who stood to one side watching everything unfold.

“Have you completely lost your mind?” Robert said, looking at Martin with contempt. “You are supposed to watch over your charges. That is your task.”

Martin, plainly embarrassed, tried to justify himself, but Robert did not allow him to speak.

“Get out,” he ordered sharply. “You and your bodyguards will guard the fortress as punishment for your stupid behavior.”

After that, Robert summoned Oksana for questioning. He looked at her with a serious expression, demanding details about her life and about what she knew of the obituaries. Oksana gathered her thoughts and answered every question. After the interrogation, Robert finally allowed her to leave.

“You may advertise your concert,” he said, and a note of approval sounded in his voice.

Oksana was overjoyed. She thanked Robert and left his office full of enthusiasm.

Left alone, Robert sank into thoughts of his wife. He often remembered her, her advice, and wondered: What if she is alive? Would she like the fortress? Would she approve of the world and the society I am trying to create?

At that moment Natalia, the cellist, unexpectedly entered the office. She looked at Robert in silence, and he, losing himself in the depth of her eyes, for a moment forgot the wife for whom he had suffered so deeply.

Natalia did not say a word, yet her presence caused Robert a pleasant dizziness. He felt his heart begin to beat faster and his thoughts grow tangled. She was so mysterious and magnetic that he could not take his eyes off her.

“Well? Who is going to take the initiative? You are the man,” Natalia said playfully, and Robert could not help smiling.

He could hardly believe his ears. At that moment Kenneth, having reached the office, pushed the door open a little and saw the scene before him: Robert and Natalia were kissing passionately. He froze in place, unable to believe his eyes.

“Oh my God,” Kenneth whispered, deciding not to interfere. He understood that it was none of his business, and that it was better to leave them alone.

Suddenly Luis appeared beside him.

“Natalia! Are you here?” Luis exclaimed, and Robert and Natalia were unable to continue their kiss.

Robert quickly gathered himself and pretended that nothing had happened. He pulled away from Natalia and tried to recover his composure.

“Luis, what are you doing here?” Robert asked, trying to sound casual.

Before Luis could answer, Kenneth stepped in, trying to reduce the tension. Yet the atmosphere had already changed. Luis looked at Natalia, then at Robert, and his face hardened.

“I came to look for Natalia,” he said coldly. “And it seems I found more than I expected.”

Natalia remained silent. Her gaze was distant, as though she herself did not fully understand what had just occurred. Robert felt irritation rise inside him, but beneath it there was shame, desire, and confusion.

“This is not your concern,” Robert said.

“Everything that concerns Natalia concerns me,” Luis replied. “You have a wife, Robert.”

The words struck Robert with painful force. He clenched his jaw and turned away for a moment. The memory of his wife returned, bright and merciless. Then he looked again at Natalia the cellist and felt the old world inside him crack.

Luis did not wait for an answer. He turned and left, but not before throwing back one final sentence:

“Robert is a true scoundrel.”

Sasha remained with Kenneth. Noticing his thoughtful gaze, she asked:

“Do you need help?”

“Yes,” Kenneth answered. “I want to talk to Oksana.”

Sasha felt a sudden surge of anger.

“Go to hell.”

Kenneth was stunned when he realized that Sasha had sincere feelings for him. He understood that he himself was falling in love with Oksana, but now everything seemed complicated and tangled. Martin, too, was fascinated by Oksana, and Kenneth did not know how to handle these difficult emotions.

The moon shone brightly, lighting the corridors of the underground complex and creating an atmosphere of mystery and expectation. Kenneth gathered his thoughts and went to the room where Oksana was sleeping. He felt that this evening might become decisive in his life. He knocked on the door and waited. A moment later it opened slightly, and Natalia appeared on the threshold.

“Hello, Kenneth,” she said, a little embarrassed. Her voice was soft, but there was awkwardness in it. “I am sorry for what you saw.”

“Do not worry,” Kenneth interrupted. “It does not matter to me. I came to speak with Oksana.”

Natalia nodded, stepped aside, and let him in. Oksana, awakened by the sound, opened her eyes and smiled when she saw Kenneth. Her face brightened with joy, and rubbing her eyes, she asked:

“Kenneth, are you all right?”

“Yes, everything is fine,” he answered, trying to hide his excitement. “I have just met the love of my life.”

Oksana blushed, her cheeks turning bright pink, and Natalia, standing nearby, exclaimed in surprise:

“Oh my God!”

Kenneth ignored Natalia’s reaction and asked Oksana:

“Would you like to take a walk?”

Oksana hesitated a little, then agreed, adding:

“Only with my guard.”

Kenneth nodded, and they went outside. The night was clear, the sky bright, without a single cloud. Moonlight lit their path, creating a magical atmosphere like something from a romantic film. Kenneth felt his heart beat faster whenever he was close to Oksana.

“Let us go to another tower,” he suggested, leading her onward.

“I hope no one was killed from this one,” Oksana teased, her voice playful.

“Do not worry,” Kenneth replied with a smile. “This is my room. From here there is a beautiful view of the stars.”

When they reached the tower, Kenneth began telling Oksana about the constellations. He especially pointed out Orion, explaining its mythology and meaning. His voice was confident, and Oksana listened with interest.

“It is incredible,” she said, looking at the stars. “I have never seen them so beautiful and so close.”

“You know,” Kenneth continued, “your concert will be in two days. I have already reserved a place so I can see you on stage.”

Oksana smiled, her eyes shining with happiness. She felt that this evening had become special, and new feelings were awakening in her heart.

Meanwhile, in the room where Oksana had been sleeping, Natalia began to play Passions on the cello. The melody filled the space, and Robert, waking, recognized her music. He wanted to go to her, but he did not know how. The music was so penetrating that he could not resist its magic.

Robert, I know you can hear me. Come to me, please, Natalia thought as she sank into the music, her heart beating in unison with the melody.

Robert hesitated, not knowing what to do. At that moment Luis passed by and also heard Natalia playing. He stopped, listening, and when he noticed Robert, approached him.

“You betrayed your wife, Robert,” Luis said accusingly. “And now you have entangled yourself with Natalia, the cellist.”

Robert did not understand what was happening. He felt anger rise inside him. Luis’s words cut him like a knife, and he could not allow himself to appear vulnerable.

“If you continue treating me with disrespect and interfering in my affairs, you will regret it,” he said threateningly, his voice trembling with rage.

“Natalia is mine,” Luis replied, refusing to back down. “She is the love of my life. Leave her alone.”

As she played Passions, Natalia felt the tension in the air and continued playing without breaking away from the music, as though calling Robert closer. The melody became more and more emotional, and Robert felt fury boil within him. He could not believe that Luis dared to speak to him that way, and his anger only grew.

Meanwhile Sasha, watching Kenneth and Oksana from a distance, thought: Why did that idiot Robert allow these witches into our fortress? Her heart filled with resentment and jealousy. At that moment Martin approached her.

“Sasha, can we talk?” he asked, and there was a seriousness in his voice that caught her attention.

Sasha turned to him, her expression changing, and nodded. She knew a conversation with Martin might be important, and perhaps he could help her understand the situation.

“What happened?” she asked, trying to hide her emotions.

“I noticed that you are worried,” Martin began. “Is it because of Kenneth and Oksana?”

Sasha sighed. Her thoughts rushed between anger and concern. She could not understand how Kenneth could become so quickly fascinated by Oksana, and it made her feel betrayed.

“Yes,” she admitted. “I simply do not understand how he could forget us so quickly.”

“Perhaps he is only looking for happiness,” Martin suggested. “We all deserve to be happy, even if it means someone else may take our place.”

Sasha shook her head, unwilling to accept his words. She felt it was not merely a coincidence, that something larger lay behind everything.

“I cannot simply sit and watch him go,” she said firmly. “I have to do something.”

Martin looked at her with understanding.

“I will support you,” he said. “But remember, sometimes the best thing is simply to let go.”

Sasha was not sure she could do that. Emotions raged in her heart, and she knew she had to act if she wanted to win Kenneth back.

Meanwhile, on the tower, Kenneth and Oksana continued enjoying the moment. Kenneth, feeling warmth fill his heart, looked at Oksana as moonlight illuminated her face.

“You know,” he said, “I always dreamed of an evening like this. You make it special.”

Oksana smiled, her eyes shining with happiness.

“I feel it too,” she confessed. “I never thought I could meet someone who understands me the way you do.”

Kenneth, unable to restrain his feelings, leaned closer and said quietly:

“I want you to know that you matter to me.”

Moved by his words, Oksana felt her heart beat faster. She knew that a special connection had arisen between them, and that it was something more than simple attraction.

Below, the tension between Robert and Luis continued to grow. Robert, unable to restrain his emotions, stepped forward.

“You have no right to tell me what to do!” he shouted, his voice echoing through the corridor.

Luis did not retreat.

“I will not step back. Natalia deserves better than you.”

From above, Oksana noticed that the tension between Robert and Luis had reached its limit. She quickly descended and intervened to stop the conflict.

“Stop!” she exclaimed, her voice confident and commanding. “We should not quarrel among ourselves. This is not what we need now. Instead, I invite both of you to my concert. It will be an opportunity for all of us to unite and forget our disagreements, even if only for a while.”

Robert and Luis, still full of emotion, froze and looked at Oksana. Her proposal sounded like a lifeline in a stormy sea. They exchanged glances, and finally Robert nodded.

“Very well,” he said, trying to calm himself. “I accept your invitation. Music may truly help us find common ground.”

Luis, still somewhat angry but understanding that Oksana was right, also agreed.

“Yes. Let us try. Music may be what we need.”

Oksana smiled, and her eyes shone with joy.

“Excellent. I am sure it will be an unforgettable evening.”

After that everyone went to their rooms, but tension still lingered in the air. Robert, returning to his room, could not free himself from thoughts of Natalia and of how his life had changed. He felt that his dreams of peace and safety were still far from reality.

In another part of the fortress, Martin and Sasha remained alone. They sat on the roof, looking at the stars and discussing the recent events.

“Did you notice how easily Oksana entered our lives?” Martin asked, his voice full of dissatisfaction. “It is too suspicious.”

Sasha thought for a moment and nodded.

“Yes. I feel it too. She is too good to be true. And her arrival coincided with the moment we began rebuilding our fortress.”

“I think we must be careful,” Martin continued, his tone becoming more insistent. “Perhaps she and her people have plans of their own. We cannot allow them to destroy what we have built.”

Sasha felt determination fill her heart.

“We must find out what they are planning. I do not trust them, and I cannot allow them to use us for their own purposes.”

Martin noticed her vulnerability and decided to use it.

“You are right, Sasha. But you know I have always been on your side. If you want, I will help you watch them. We can do it together.”

Sasha felt supported by his words, though uncertainty remained deep inside her.

“All right. Let us watch them,” she agreed, although her intuition told her Martin might have motives of his own.

Seeing that Sasha trusted him, Martin smiled to himself.

“We must stay alert and not allow them to deceive us,” he said, his voice confident.

They exchanged glances filled with understanding and determination, but concern still lingered in Sasha’s eyes. In that moment, she did not realize that her alliance with Martin might become not only a protection, but also a trap into which she was leading herself.
