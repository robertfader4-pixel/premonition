The fortress

Utopia is not a place without danger. It is a dream built where danger still breathes.

In a fractured world, Robert searches for survivors who still believe that trust can be stronger than fear. Yet every wall he builds is tested by the passions that once destroyed the old world — desire, pride, grief, devotion, and the fierce need to survive.
